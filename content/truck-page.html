WORKING TORQUE · REAL OPERATING COSTS

More useful pull.
A measured approach.

Truck eco tuning adjusts the ECU calibration around the engine’s working range. The aim is to improve loaded drivability and help the truck maintain momentum with fewer unnecessary gear changes.

The right approach starts with the engine, gearbox, mechanical condition and duty cycle. Fuel economy depends on how that combination performs on your actual routes.

  • Torque delivery suited to heavy loads and everyday working speeds.
  • Coordinated fuel, boost and torque requests for the specific vehicle.
  • Assessment of existing faults before calibration.
  • Before-and-after fuel records to evaluate the result.

RELIABILITY & DIAGNOSTICS

Warning lights?
Start with the cause.

DPF, AdBlue/SCR and engine-management faults can interrupt work through warnings or reduced power. Include the fault codes, symptoms and any previous repairs in your enquiry so Pieter can discuss the appropriate assessment.

Existing injector, turbo, boost-leak or engine-condition problems need attention before economy tuning. Software should be part of a considered solution for the specific truck.
