THE INVESTMENT BEHIND THE RESULT

Good software starts
long before the flash.

We have invested heavily in the equipment, workshop facilities and development time needed to understand what a calibration actually does.

At Boostech Bloemfontein, our own calibrated dynamometer is part of that process. It gives us a controlled environment to measure output, assess the shape of the torque curve and refine the way an engine delivers its performance.

In-house development means working through the details: identifying the ECU and software version, understanding the vehicle’s hardware, evaluating changes and checking the result. The goal is useful performance you can feel in everyday driving, towing and touring.

Our local development is supported by the wider Boostech network’s shared experience across engines, software versions and operating conditions.
